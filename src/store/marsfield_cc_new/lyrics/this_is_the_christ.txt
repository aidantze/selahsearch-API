This is the Christ that we proclaim 
The One who set the stars in place 
Who holds all things within his hand
Awakens life at his command

This is our God, who left his throne
A human frame became his home 
He felt our grief and knew our pain 
This is the Christ that we proclaim 

This is the Christ that we proclaim 
The Son who came to seek and save 
He bore our sin upon the tree 
And died the death reserved for me 

This is our God, who crushed the curse 
As Adam's failure he reversed 
He rose in triumph from the grave
This is the Christ that we proclaim 

This is the Christ that we proclaim 
To tell the world of Jesus' name 
Until the day when every tongue 
Declares the glory of the Son

This is our God, on heaven's throne 
He will return to claim his own 
And we will sing for endless days: 
"This Is the Christ that we proclaim!"
